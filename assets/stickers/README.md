# Albumium sticker assets

## Original Albumium artwork

The keepsake artwork in this directory was generated specifically for Albumium
on 2026-08-30 from original, brand-free prompts. It does not contain
third-party logos, characters, typography, or downloaded marketplace artwork.

The visual research used only general scrapbook traditions and openly reusable
public-domain collection categories (pressed botany, travel ephemera, and
decorative objects). No external source image is bundled in these assets.

Generation prompts intentionally requested transparent backgrounds and no
text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, flags, or recognizable branded
imagery.

## Expanded keepsake collection

Eight additional images were generated for Albumium with the built-in image
generation workflow on 2026-08-30. The four original keepsakes were used only
as style references. No Pinterest, marketplace, stock, CC0, or other
third-party image file is included.

The common production prompt requested one cohesive premium scrapbook cutout,
photorealistic tactile paper/fabric/metal textures, a genuinely transparent
background, and no text, letters, numbers, logos, brands, people, hands, or
watermarks. Subject variants:

- `photo_keepsake.webp`: unbranded camera, blank instant photos, film and brass
  accents.
- `music_keepsake.webp`: unbranded record, cassette, headphones, blank ticket
  and velvet ribbon.
- `sewing_keepsake.webp`: vintage buttons, brass scissors, thread, lace, safety
  pin and gingham.
- `celebration_keepsake.webp`: silver disco ball, velvet ribbon, stars, confetti
  and an unnumbered candle.
- `seaside_keepsake.webp`: shells, starfish, pearls, pale ribbon, tiny bottle and
  weathered map paper.
- `cafe_keepsake.webp`: unbranded floral cup, croissant, spoon, dried daisy,
  gingham and handmade paper.
- `friendship_keepsake.webp`: intertwined friendship bracelets, heart charm,
  pressed daisies, pearls and ribbon.
- `cozy_reading_keepsake.webp`: blank linen notebook, reading glasses, ceramic
  cocoa cup, knit ribbon, leaves and brass key.

All final assets were checked for an alpha channel before being added to the
app. The 12 keepsakes are shipped as full-resolution WebP files encoded at
quality 88 with lossless alpha. This reduces their installed payload by about
82% while preserving the source dimensions and natural aspect ratios.
